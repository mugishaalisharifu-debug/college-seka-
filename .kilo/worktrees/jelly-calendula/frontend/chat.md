# College Foundation Sina Gerard (CFSG) School Management System

## Overview

The College Foundation Sina Gerard School Management System is a digital platform designed to help the school manage its daily operations in an organized, secure, and efficient way. It connects the public-facing school website with a private staff dashboard so that different departments can work together from one system.

The system is built to support the entire student journey, from application and admission to registration, payment, academic tracking, discipline monitoring, and reporting.

---

## Main Purpose of the System

The system helps the school to:

- manage student applications and admissions
- track student requirements and enrollment records
- handle school fees and payments
- support academic and class management
- record attendance and discipline cases
- manage reports, documents, announcements, and gallery content
- give each user role access to the tools relevant to their job

---

## System Structure

The system has two main parts:

1. Public/Marketing Side
   - school information pages
   - admission information
   - contact, news, gallery, downloads, and program details

2. Staff Dashboard
   - secure login for different school users
   - role-based access to the correct modules
   - reports and records management
   - school operations support for administrators and departments

---

## User Roles and Responsibilities

### 1. System Administrator
The System Administrator is the highest-level user in the system. This role controls the overall setup of the platform and ensures that all departments are working properly.

Responsibilities:
- login to the system
- view the administrator dashboard
- configure school information
- configure academic years and terms
- publish announcements
- manage gallery items
- upload downloadable documents
- generate and export reports

### 2. Headmaster
The Headmaster oversees the school’s academic and administrative activities. This role is responsible for reviewing student applications and ensuring that the school is running smoothly.

Responsibilities:
- login to the system
- view the headmaster dashboard
- review enrolled students
- view reports
- export reports

### 3. Administrative Staff
Administrative Staff handle student applications before they reach the Headmaster. They ensure that applications are complete and that required information and documents are provided.

Responsibilities:
- login to the system
- view new applications
- verify application information
- verify uploaded documents
- update applicant information
- forward verified applications
- manage parent information

### 4. School Reception Officer
The School Reception Officer works with approved students after admission has been approved. This role checks that all required documents and school materials have been received before registration is completed.

Responsibilities:
- login to the system
- view approved students only
- search students by name or admission number
- open student profile
- verify birth certificate
- verify previous school report
- verify passport photos
- verify medical report
- verify school uniform
- verify sports uniform
- verify boarding requirements
- verify TVET practical equipment
- mark requirements as received or not received
- add remarks for missing items
- update requirement status
- print requirement checklist
- generate requirement reports

Specific role note:
- The person who adds requirements is the Director of Discipline (DOD).
- The DOD is responsible for recording and checking the student requirements before final registration is completed.

### 5. School Bursar
The School Bursar manages all financial activities in the school, including fee payments and financial records.

Responsibilities:
- login to the system
- view enrolled students
- search student records
- view student fee profile
- record fee payments
- select academic year and school term
- generate payment receipts
- update remaining balances
- view payment history
- generate and export financial reports

### 6. Director of Studies (DOS)
The Director of Studies manages academic operations such as classes, teacher assignment, curriculum activities, examinations, and academic reporting.

Responsibilities:
- login to the system
- manage academic years
- manage school terms
- create classes
- manage TVET trades
- assign teachers
- view student academic records
- record examination results
- monitor curriculum coverage
- generate and export academic reports
- review applications
- approve or reject applications

### 7. Director of Discipline (DOD)
The Director of Discipline is responsible for managing student attendance and discipline matters across the school.

Responsibilities:
- login to the system
- view classes
- record attendance
- mark present, absent, or late
- record absence reasons
- record discipline cases
- update discipline records
- generate and export discipline reports

### 8. Other Support Roles in the Dashboard
The system also supports other departments through separate dashboard areas such as:
- Cashier
- Requirement Collector
- Inventory and stock management staff
- Waiter-related operational modules

These roles help the school manage daily operational activities in a structured way.

## Admission and Enrollment Workflow

The school management process follows a clear workflow:

1. Student submits an online application.
2. Administrative Staff verifies the application details.
3. Headmaster reviews the verified application.
4. The Headmaster approves or rejects the application.
5. If approved, the student is assigned to a class.
6. The approved student appears in the Reception Officer dashboard.
7. The Reception Officer checks all required documents and materials.
8. Missing requirements are recorded.
9. The student completes the missing requirements.
10. The School Bursar records fee payments.
11. The Director of Studies manages academic information.
12. The Director of Discipline records attendance and discipline information.
13. The student becomes an officially enrolled member of the school.

---

## Summary

The CFSG School Management System is designed to make school administration easier, faster, and more transparent. It helps each department do its work efficiently while keeping student records, payments, academic information, and discipline records organized in one system.

This system is not only a record-keeping platform, but also a complete workflow tool for managing the school from admission to enrollment and beyond.
